# Agentic coding usage

OpenCode was used to develop the static app, research dataset licenses, implement the R workflow, and test the interface. The author chose the teaching scope and approved the switch to the explicitly licensed 10x datasets.
Checks included barcode alignment, disjoint donor roles, repeat SingleR computation, TSV-to-R comparison and browser inspection. Earlier candidates were rejected for unusable expression or less clear redistribution evidence. No results were fabricated.
